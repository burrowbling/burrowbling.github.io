about the game
After a long winter, your home, the Cascadia Islands, are in ruins. Plant and grow flowers to repair objects, clear weeds and sell them at the docks, zipline around town and buy new tools and decor, and catch dragonflies in rich meadows and forests.
MUDBIRD's playtime is around 1-2 hours and comes with lots of plants, lots of sitting, weather events, swimming, fishing, and more.
