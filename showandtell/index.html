About

what do i bring?
You should bring something of importance to you.
Try not to bring something you made.
You can bring an artwork, image, short video or audio piece, or poem. Or, you can bring a physical object, like a rock, bowl, or sculpture. If you want, you can even show a dance or another cool trick/skill.

what is a "show and tell?"
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Show_and_tell

more info
Show & Tell happens mostly every other Sunday and is an event series brought to you by Saltwater People, a Providence-based design collective. You can reach out for contact at samtoeman@gmail.com.
