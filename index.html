Samantha Toeman is a nineteen year old artist and designer from New York studying at the Rhode Island School of Design. She's preparing for the fall by riding her bike around, thinking about restaurant branding, drinking lots of coffee, and playing old records on her roommate's record player. Contact her at samtoeman(at)gmail(dot)com.

Her favorite things right now are the word 'pleasant', old school coffee grinders, American-style ice cream, and the promise of a better internet.
